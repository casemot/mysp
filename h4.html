There are exactly ten occurrences of the word Yeshua ישוע across the L_B (Deu.5.7-21) with "constant letter-jump": nine times from right to left and one time backwards. This last occurrence of the word Yeshua (backwards: עושי ) crosses another word in the middle, namely the only occurrence of the word Messiah משיח (at L_B(327,272,217,162) ) in L_B also in reverse order along the grid (חישמ).
There is two other occurrences in L_A (Ex.20.3-17) of the Hebrew name of Jesus: Yeshua (which brings a total of 12 times the word Yeshua in total for both versions of the Laws).

Interestingly enough, when one knows that the letter vaw (ו) means "nail" in Hebrew (as explained here: www.juliasneedledesigns.com/HebrewAlphabet.html and aside from the fact that it's also the number 6), it could be relevant to notice that nearby the word Yeshua, starting from the letter shin (ש), three letters to the right, three letters to the left and finally starting from the letter ayin (ע), three letters to the bottom you have each time the letter vaw. Hence we find a cross having an horizontal and a vertical bar of both seven letters within this width 77 grid appearing right here next to the center of the Law (which is the letter ה at position L_B(334) as already seen). Indeed the (7x7) square of 49 letters starting at position L_B(178) (as right upper corner) with the letter ש contains both the name of Yeshua and three nails forming a cross in this square which is centered one letter away from the center of L_B(334).
