Interestingly enough, the word “truth” , in hebrew : אמת , appears exactly 119 times in L_A and 126 times in L_B ; in fact, 60 times from right to left and 59 times in reverse order accross L_A and analogeously, 77 and 49 times in L_B. As comparing with the word "Sabbath" , which appears 7x7 times, the word אמת (or תמא) appears 7x17 times in L_A and 7x18 times in L_B , noticing that 17 is p(7) (where p(n) designates the nth prime number) thus we have in L_A exactly 7 x p(7) occurences of אמת and 7 x (p(7)+1) occurrences in L_B.
