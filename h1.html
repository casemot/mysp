This type of analysis is actually quite simple; it answers the question of how many times a word can be read vertically (without spaces, from bottom to top or vice versa) by varying the size of the grid containing the text being analyzed. In other words, we count in the following case the number of times the word שבת appears by keeping its letters equidistant. We do that across the two versions of the law (L_A and L_B). Surprisingly, this number is identical (49) although the first version has 579 letters and the second version has 667.
