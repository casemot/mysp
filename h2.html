By the way, in hebrew, ה (center of L_B) is also the number 5 and ב (center of L_A) is also the number 2 which add up to 7 (and corresponds to 5 words in normal reading direction plus two words in counter-direction).

Lastly, in L_A the word "Shabbat" appears in plain text whereas in L_B the word "tohu" appears well in the center of the entire text but as the last two letters of the word אתה  and the first letter of the word ובנך which litterally mean "you and your son" (therefore the word "tohu" escapes the normal reading view).

(Furthermore, the word "Sabbath," which was in the exact center of L_A, is also found in L_B, but there is exactly 26 letters between the center of L_B and the center of the second occurence of this word in L_B; incidentally, 26 is the arithmetic mean of 23 and 29).
